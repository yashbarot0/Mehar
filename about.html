Our Founder

Milan Brahmbhatt

Founder, Mehar Associates · B.Tech (Civil)

Milan Brahmbhatt founded Mehar Associates in 1998. A civil engineer by training, he has spent the 28 years since building in Nadiad: homes, apartments and commercial spaces for more than 500 families and businesses.

Under his leadership the firm follows ISO 9001:2015 quality management practices, and every project it has launched has been fully sold out. That record rests on his hands-on approach: sound engineering, honest advice and staying involved from the first drawing to handing over the keys.

"Committed to Trust" is not just our tagline. It's how we've worked for 28 years.
